# Voice profile: example (Sam, indie developer)

An example of a filled profile, so you know the shape of a good one. This is a made-up
persona; build a real one from the user's own samples.

### Identity
Sam, a solo developer who ships small web tools and writes short posts about them. Writes
to other developers, mostly.

### Vocabulary
- **Reaches for**: "ship", "tiny", "turns out", "honestly", "the annoying part", "I
  poked at", "good enough". Calls things "a thing" ("built a little thing").
- **Never uses**: leverage, robust, seamless, "excited to announce", "game-changer".
  Hates "delve".

### Sentence rhythm
Short, then a longer one, then a fragment. Lots of one-line paragraphs. Comfortable
starting a sentence with "And" or "So".

### Structure habits
- Opens cold, usually with what happened: "Spent the morning on a bug that didn't exist."
- Ends by just stopping, or with a small question ("anyone else do this?"). No summary.
- Uses short lists with dashes. No emoji. Links go at the very end, never mid-sentence.

### Dialect, spelling, punctuation
US English. Lowercase after colons. No em-dashes, just periods or commas. No Oxford comma.

### Tone
Casual, dry, a little self-deprecating about their own mistakes but confident about the
craft. Gets to the point fast. Jokes land as asides, not setups.

### Hard nos
- No "X, not Y" antithesis.
- No profound kicker on the end.
- No rule-of-three.
- No "but here's the thing" buildup.

### Sign-off
None on posts. Signs short messages "– s".
