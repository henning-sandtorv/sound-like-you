# Voice profile: template and builder

A voice profile captures how one person actually writes, so an AI can draft as them.
This file is the schema and the procedure. The live profile lives in `voice-profile.md`.

## How to build a profile from samples

1. Ask the user for **3 to 5 real samples** of their own writing. Things they wrote and
   sent, not co-written or heavily edited. A mix is best: a message, a post, an email.
2. Read for **patterns, not topics**. You're after how they write, not what about.
3. Fill in every section below from evidence in the samples. Quote real fragments.
   Where the samples don't show something, write "unknown, ask" rather than guessing.
4. Write the result to `voice-profile.md`, show it to the user, and adjust until they
   say it sounds right. Re-run whenever they give you new samples or corrections.

## Schema

### Identity
Who this is, and what they tend to write (so the voice has a context). One or two lines.

### Vocabulary
- **Reaches for**: words and phrases they use often. Quote real ones from the samples.
- **Never uses**: words that would sound wrong in their mouth (include any from
  `ai-tells.md` they'd especially hate).

### Sentence rhythm
- Typical length, and how much it varies. Short and punchy? Long and winding? Lumpy?
- Do they use fragments? Run-ons? One-line paragraphs?

### Structure habits
- How they open (cold fact? story? question?).
- How they end (just stop? dry aside? a real question to the reader?).
- Lists, headers, emoji, links: yes or no, and how.

### Dialect, spelling, punctuation
- Dialect or regional spelling, if any. Capitalization habits. Comma style.
- Em-dash / semicolon / ellipsis use. Oxford comma or not.

### Tone
- Formal or casual. How much they hedge. Do they joke? Self-deprecate? Get straight to it?
- How they come across: blunt, warm, dry, precise.

### Hard nos
- Constructions this person refuses: the tells from `ai-tells.md` plus anything personal.
  These are non-negotiable in every draft.

### Sign-off
- How they close a message or post, if they have a habit.

## Notes
- A profile is a description, not a costume. Don't exaggerate the dialect or tics into a
  caricature. Match the real frequency.
- Keep it short enough to hold in context. A page is plenty.
